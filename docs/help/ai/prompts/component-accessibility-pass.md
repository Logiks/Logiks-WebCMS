Review the component [[component name]] for accessibility and fix what you find.
Check: heading levels; alt text; link and button names that make sense out of context; contrast of text and controls (4.5:1 for body text); visible keyboard focus; touch targets at least 44 px;
reduced-motion respected; no information carried by colour alone; the page works at 375 px and zoomed to 200%.
List each problem with its fix, apply the fixes, then show me the preview.
