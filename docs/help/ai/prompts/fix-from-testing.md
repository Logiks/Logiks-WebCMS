Open the latest AI Testing report. Take the findings with severity [[error / warning]] on [[page or all pages]] and fix them one at a time, starting with the ones that affect the most pages.
After each fix say what the cause was and what you changed. Do not change content or design beyond what the finding needs. When finished re-check those pages and list what remains.
