I am starting a website. Do not build anything yet. Interview me and write a brief.

About us: [[Organisation and what it does]]
Who visits and what they want: [[Audience]]
The one thing a visitor should do: [[Main action, e.g. call, book, buy, subscribe]]
Tone: [[Three words, e.g. warm, plain, trustworthy]]
Brand: [[Colours, logo, fonts, or "none yet"]]
Reference sites I like (and what I like about each): [[Links and notes]]
Must-have features: [[Blog, shop, booking, members area, languages]]

Ask me at most seven questions in ONE message, each with the default you would choose, for anything that would change the result.
Then save the answers and your stated assumptions in the requirements document under the heading "Brief and assumptions", and show me a half-minute summary.
Do not invent facts about us. Mark anything unknown as "to be supplied by the owner".
